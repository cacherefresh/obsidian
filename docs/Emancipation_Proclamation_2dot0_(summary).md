# Emancipation Proclamation 2.0  
### A Unified Appeal for Federal Cannabis Reform, Systemic Justice, and Public Health

---

**To:**  
- The 50 United States Governors  
- Members of the House of Representatives  
- Executive Branch 

---

## Subject  
**A Threefold Proposal to Federally Decriminalize Cannabis and Free Nonviolent Offenders**  
*Under the Authority of Article V, House Leadership (We the People), and Executive Deregulation*

---

## Key National Data & Constitutional Impact – Cannabis Legalization Snapshot

### U.S. State Cannabis Legalization Overview
- 38 states and 3 territories + D.C. have legalized **medical marijuana**
- 24 states and 2 territories + D.C. have legalized **adult-use recreational marijuana**
- 14+ additional states have enacted **decriminalization measures**

### Incarceration & Cost
- Over **31,000 individuals** remain incarcerated for nonviolent cannabis offenses  
- **$1.1 billion** annual taxpayer burden for incarceration related to cannabis  
- **$600 million** annual court system sub-burden (clogged dockets, hearings, public defense)

### Constitutional Relevance
> The **God Given** *(not government authorized)*  **Right to Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness**
> The **Right to Privacy**, as implied in the **Fourth** and **Ninth Amendments**, is infringed by warrantless raids, over-policing, and disproportionate cannabis enforcement.
> The **Second Amendment** as well as **Voting Rights**, **Abolition of Slavery**...(the list goes on)
> 
### Estimated Federal Savings & Revenue Boost
- **$1.1B+** in annual incarceration savings  
- **$4.2B+** in 2023 state-level cannabis tax revenue  
- Potential **$10B+** annual federal revenue from full legalization and interstate commerce

### Economic Participation & Workforce Restoration
- Transitioning nonviolent cannabis offenders into the workforce:  
  - Average federal income tax per worker: **~$8,500/year**  
  - Estimated reentry: **25,000–30,000 citizens**  
  - Additional payroll, sales, and housing tax contributions across states

---

## Triage and Justice Integrity Measures

I have taken the time to thoroughly consider the **downstream ramifications of mass release**.  
I recognize that some violent offenders may have taken plea deals that reduced their charges to nonviolent cannabis possession or distribution. Therefore:

### Immediate Actions
All individuals currently incarcerated **solely for minor cannabis possession or distribution** should receive **immediate clemency and expungement**, supported by:
- Former President **Donald Trump’s** federal pardon authority  
- **DEA** descheduling of cannabis  
- **Department of Homeland Security** and **Health and Human Services** deregulation under **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.**

### Case-by-Case Reviews
Cases with additional or violent charges must be:
- Reviewed quickly but **methodically** on a **case-by-case basis**
- Evaluated to **distinguish genuine nonviolent offenders** from those who pled down from serious crimes

### Public Safety Oversight
To protect public safety while advancing justice:
- A **national anonymous tip line** should be established to report known violent offenders released under false pretenses  
- Oversight should include:
  - Public defenders  
  - Parole officers  
  - Civil rights organizations  
  - Former inmates  

This is not just about **freedom**—it’s about **responsibility**.  
We can **free the innocent without endangering the public**, and we must.

---

## Letter to Lawmakers

**Dear Public Servants, Lawmakers, and Leaders of Conscience,**

I submit this letter under **three parallel banners:**

1. A **call for a Convention of States** under *Article V* to address the federal criminalization of cannabis and its cascading societal harms.  
2. A **proposal for Congressional consideration** through a surgical bill (not an omnibus), brought to the floor by a Speaker elected in alignment with the will of the people — *symbolically or officially.*  
3. A **deregulatory petition** to the *Department of Health and Human Services*, aligning with the spirit and intention of **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.**’s invitation for systemic healing and public input
